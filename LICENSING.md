| Activity | License treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Use, modify and fork for noncommercial purposes | Permitted |
| Share copies and modifications for permitted purposes | Permitted; include the license or its URL and required notices |
| Personal study, hobby projects and research without anticipated commercial application | Explicitly permitted |
| Use by educational institutions, public research organizations and the other organizations listed in the license | Explicitly permitted regardless of funding source or funding obligations |
| Commercial resale, paid hosted access or incorporation into a commercial product | No general permission is granted; separate authorization is needed unless a license permission applies |
| Other commercial use, including internal business use | No general permission is granted; separate authorization is needed unless a license permission applies |

The restriction concerns commercial purposes, not just whether someone reports
a net profit. Free distribution does not automatically make a commercial use
permitted. Conversely, the license explicitly allows the listed institutional
uses regardless of funding. It is not a blanket prohibition on every activity
involving money.

This is **source-available software with noncommercial restrictions**, rather
than OSI-defined open-source software. Open-source licenses permit commercial
use. The complete [license](LICENSE) governs; this guide explains it and adds no
new restrictions or permissions.

## Sharing your adaptations

Include [LICENSE](LICENSE), [NOTICE](NOTICE), and applicable third-party notices
when sharing a copy or fork. The license also permits providing its URL instead
of a copy of its text, with the required notices. Clearly identifying your own
changes helps recipients distinguish your version from the maintained project.

Contributions submitted for inclusion in this repository must be offered under
the same PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, except for identified third-party
material accepted under its own compatible terms. Contributors retain their
copyright; submission does not assign ownership or grant an additional right
to relicense their contribution. See [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Third-party components and user documents

Bundled libraries, fonts, decoders and other third-party material retain their
own licenses. The root noncommercial restriction does not replace or narrow
those upstream grants. Preserve their license files and notices; the
[vendor guide](static/vendor/README.md) identifies bundled browser components.
Other files with explicit third-party license notices retain those terms too.

Using the application does not place your manuscripts, bibliography entries,
PDFs, annotations or other user-created documents under the application license.
Rights in those documents remain with their respective rights holders. Copies
of application code or third-party assets included in an output retain their
applicable terms.
